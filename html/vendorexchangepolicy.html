E-commerce laws in India are a mix of specific rules and existing laws covering IT, consumer rights, taxes, and FDI, with key regulations including the IT Act, 2000 (digital signatures, cyber security), the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (transparency, grievance redressal, no pre-tick return, refund, and exchange policy boxes). Other crucial laws are the GST Act, 2017 for taxation, the FDI policy (limiting inventory models for FDI), and the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (packaging/labelling). These frameworks aim to ensure fair practices, data protection, and clear accountability for platforms and sellers.
