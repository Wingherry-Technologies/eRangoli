Threads of Tradition: Frame Loom Weaving with Master Artisans

by Manohar Khake

Category : Weaving Workshop    Tags: Uttar Pradesh

About the Workshop

Step into the world of handcrafted textiles and discover the timeless art of Frame Loom Weaving guided by expert artisans. This hands-on workshop introduces participants to the fundamentals of weaving, traditional techniques, and creative textile exploration.

Whether you are a beginner, design student, or craft enthusiast, this session will help you understand the beauty of warp and weft while creating your own woven masterpiece.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to frame loom tools and materials
  • Understanding warp & weft basics
  • Setting up a frame loom
  • Basic weaving techniques and patterns
  • Color combinations and texture creation
  • Finishing and securing your woven piece

What You Will Create

Each participant will design and complete a small woven wall hanging or textile sample to take home.

Who Can Join

  • School & college students
  • Design and architecture students
  • Craft lovers & hobbyists
  • Anyone interested in handmade textiles
  • No prior experience required.

Workshop Highlights

  • Learn directly from skilled artisans
  • Hands-on guided practice
  • All materials provided
  • Certificate of participation
  • Showcase opportunity for selected student works

Duration

3–4 Hours (Single Day Workshop)

Takeaway

Gain practical weaving skills, understand traditional craftsmanship, and experience the joy of creating something handmade from scratch. Certificate provided after completion.

FAQ

We provide all the necessary materials including yarn, frame looms, weaving needles, and finishing tools. You don't need to bring anything.

Hand wash gently in cold water using mild detergent. Lay flat to dry and avoid wringing or machine washing to preserve the texture and weave integrity.

You can reach out to us through our contact page or email us directly. We welcome collaborations with designers, institutions, and craft enthusiasts.
