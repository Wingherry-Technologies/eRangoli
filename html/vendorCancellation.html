E commerce refund and cancellation Policy

Select Language

An e-commerce refund and cancellation policy outlines rules for order changes/returns, allowing cancellations before dispatch, with refunds processed to the original payment method for eligible items (unused, original packaging, within a set timeframe, e.g., 1–10 days). Key aspects include cancellation windows (before shipment), return conditions (original state, packaging), refund timelines, legal compliance (e.g., India's CPA 2019 for defective goods), and exceptions (final sale, digital goods).

Timeframe to cancel (e.g., a few hours after order, before dispatch).

Conditions for returns (unused, original packaging, tags on) and specific reasons (defective, wrong item).

Time for the customers to return the item (e.g., 1–15 days) and how long refunds take (e.g., 1–2 weeks).

Final sale items, digital products, or completed services often have stricter rules or no refunds.

Legal Considerations (Example: India)

Consumer Protection Act, 2019: Prohibits refusing returns for defective or non-conforming products and mandates a process for redressal.

Seller Responsibility: Sellers must have clear policies; "no refund/no return" slogans are generally invalid for faulty goods.
